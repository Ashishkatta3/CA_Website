Our Services

Audit, taxation, accounting, corporate law, regulatory and finance services for businesses and individuals, all from one team.

Audit & Assurance Income Tax GST Accounting & Payroll Company Law & Governance RBI & FEMA Project & Corporate Finance Business Setup Advisory & Virtual CFO

Audit & Assurance

An audit is more than a statutory requirement. Done well, it gives management, lenders and investors confidence in your numbers. We plan and run our audits under the Standards on Auditing issued by ICAI, and we report our observations clearly so they can be acted on.

  • Statutory audit under the Companies Act, 2013
  • Tax audit under the Income-tax Act
  • Internal audit & risk-based reviews
  • Concurrent, stock & bank branch audits
  • Audit of trusts, societies & co-operatives
  • Internal financial controls (IFC) testing
  • Due diligence reviews
  • Certification & attestation services

Income Tax

We help individuals and businesses meet their obligations under the Income-tax Act, 2025, which replaced the 1961 Act from 1 April 2026, while keeping their tax cost as low as the law allows. We handle routine compliance and represent clients in complex litigation.

  • Income tax return filing (individuals, firms, companies, trusts)
  • Tax planning & advance tax computation
  • TDS / TCS compliance & quarterly returns
  • Replies to notices & faceless assessments
  • Appeals before CIT(A) and ITAT
  • NRI taxation & DTAA advisory
  • Capital gains planning & property transactions
  • Transfer pricing documentation

Goods & Services Tax (GST)

GST replaced service tax, VAT, excise and several other indirect taxes in 2017. It needs month-on-month attention to detail. We handle end-to-end compliance, keep input tax credit reconciled and protected, and represent clients in departmental proceedings.

  • GST registration, amendment & cancellation
  • GSTR-1, GSTR-3B & QRMP returns
  • Annual return (GSTR-9) & reconciliation (GSTR-9C)
  • Input tax credit reconciliation with GSTR-2B
  • Refunds for exports & inverted duty structure
  • E-invoicing & e-way bill advisory
  • Replies to notices, audits & show-cause notices
  • Legacy service tax & VAT matters

Accounting, Bookkeeping & Payroll

Accurate books are the foundation of every return, audit and business decision. We keep your accounts up to date and ready for compliance, so you can focus on running the business.

  • Bookkeeping on Tally, Zoho Books & other platforms
  • Monthly MIS & management reports
  • Finalisation of accounts under Ind AS / AS
  • Bank, vendor & customer reconciliations
  • Payroll processing & payslips
  • PF, ESI & professional tax compliance
  • Budgeting & cash-flow forecasting
  • Business process outsourcing (F&A)

Company Law & Corporate Governance

Company and LLP compliance under the Companies Act, 2013 and the LLP Act, 2008 carries strict timelines and heavy penalties for default. We manage your secretarial calendar and advise boards on good governance practice.

  • Annual ROC filings (AOC-4, MGT-7, Form 8 & 11)
  • Board & general meeting documentation
  • Maintenance of statutory registers
  • Director KYC, appointments & resignations
  • Share capital changes & allotments
  • Conversions, strike-off & closure
  • Corporate governance advisory
  • Compliance health checks

RBI & FEMA Matters

Cross-border investments and transactions must follow the Foreign Exchange Management Act and RBI directions. We advise on how to structure them and handle the reporting on the RBI's FIRMS portal and through authorised dealer banks.

  • Foreign direct investment (FDI) reporting – FC-GPR, FC-TRS
  • Overseas direct investment (ODI) filings
  • Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities & Assets (FLA)
  • External commercial borrowings (ECB)
  • Liaison / branch office compliance
  • Compounding of FEMA contraventions
  • NBFC registration & compliance
  • Form 15CA / 15CB certification

Project & Corporate Finance

We help businesses raise the right funding on the right terms, from a first term loan to working capital limits for an expanding business, with credible projections and well-documented proposals.

  • Detailed project reports (DPR)
  • CMA data & financial projections
  • Term loan & working capital proposals
  • Loan syndication with banks & NBFCs
  • Business & share valuations
  • Government subsidy & scheme applications
  • Debt restructuring advisory
  • Investment advisory

Business Setup & Registrations

Choosing the right structure at the start saves a great deal of cost and trouble later. We advise on the best entity for your goals and take care of every registration you need to start operating.

  • Private limited, OPC & LLP incorporation
  • Partnership firm & proprietorship setup
  • Section 8 companies, trusts & societies
  • 12A / 80G registration for NGOs
  • GST, PAN, TAN & professional tax
  • MSME (Udyam) & IEC registration
  • Startup India (DPIIT) recognition
  • Shops & establishments licence

Advisory & Virtual CFO

Growing businesses often need senior financial oversight before they can justify a full-time CFO. Our virtual CFO service provides that experience part-time, together with specific advisory work.

  • Virtual CFO & finance function set-up
  • Internal controls & SOP design
  • Business restructuring & succession planning
  • Cost reduction & profitability analysis
  • Investor reporting & board packs
  • Manpower management advisory

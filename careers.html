Life at the Firm

Learn by doing real work

At a mid-sized firm, you work directly with partners on real client engagements from your first week.

We look for people who are curious, disciplined and committed to quality. In return, we offer structured learning, exposure to many industries and a supportive place to grow as a professional.

  • Direct partner mentoring
  • Exposure across many industries
  • Study leave for CA exams
  • Regular knowledge sessions

CA Articleship

For CA students registered for articleship. You'll gain hands-on training in statutory and internal audits, income tax, GST and ROC compliance.

Qualified & Semi-qualified CAs

For professionals who want to lead audit and tax engagements, manage client relationships and help build new service lines.

Accounts Executives

For commerce graduates with Tally / accounting software experience, to handle bookkeeping, GST returns and payroll.

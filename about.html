Firm Profile

Anil Reddy & Co, Chartered Accountants

An established firm of Chartered Accountants working in accounts, audit, taxation, investment, RBI matters, legal and secretarial services, business process outsourcing and project financing.

The firm provides a full range of professional services, including audit, management consultancy, tax consultancy, accounting, manpower management and secretarial services.

Our team brings together Chartered Accountants, corporate financial advisors and tax consultants. Each has specialised skills, and together we give sound financial advice and personal, proactive service. We work regularly with industry and other professionals, which keeps us up to date with changes in the law and business practice.

We serve clients from two offices in Hyderabad, at KPHB Colony and Kavuri Hills, Madhapur.

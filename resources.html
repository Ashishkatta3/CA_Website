Document Checklists

What to keep ready

Individual tax return

  • PAN, Aadhaar & bank account details
  • Salary TDS certificate & payslips
  • Annual Information Statement (AIS) / Form 26AS
  • Interest certificates & capital gains statements
  • Rent, home loan & investment proofs
  • Details of foreign assets, if any

Company incorporation

  • PAN & Aadhaar of directors and shareholders
  • Passport-size photographs
  • Proof of address (bank statement / utility bill)
  • Registered office proof & NOC from owner
  • Proposed names & business objects
  • Capital structure & shareholding pattern

GST registration

  • PAN of the business
  • Proof of constitution (deed / incorporation certificate)
  • Photographs & ID of proprietor / partners / directors
  • Principal place of business proof
  • Bank account details (cancelled cheque)
  • Authorisation letter / board resolution

FAQs

Common questions

Do you work with individuals as well as companies?

Yes. We work with salaried individuals, professionals, NRIs, proprietorships, partnership firms, LLPs, private and public companies, trusts and societies.

What documents should I bring to the first meeting?

For individuals: PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 / income details and the last return filed. For businesses: incorporation documents, GST registration, the latest financial statements and any notices received. We'll send you a specific checklist once we understand your requirement.

I have received a notice from the Income Tax or GST department. Can you help?

Yes. We review the notice, prepare the reply with supporting documents and represent you before the officer. Notices have strict response deadlines, so please contact us as soon as you receive one.

How are your fees determined?

Fees depend on the scope, complexity and volume of work. We agree the fee in writing before we start, so there are no surprises.

Can you handle our work remotely?

Yes. Most compliance work, including accounting, return filing and ROC filings, can be handled through secure document sharing and video meetings, with in-person meetings at our Hyderabad offices when needed.
